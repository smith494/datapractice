# Summary & Insights: 7 Ways to Use Claude So Well It Feels Illegal

## Overview

This video presents Claude as **a full stack of AI tools** — not just a chatbot — and breaks down five core capabilities (Think, Remember, Execute, Build, Browse) into a team of agents that work together. The core thesis: the gap between people who *use* AI and people who *wield* it will reshape careers, companies, and lives over the next decade.

---

## The Five-Part AI Stack

### 1. Think — Claude Chat
- Use Chat as a **thinking partner**, not a copy-paste shortcut.
- Bad prompt: *"Write me a LinkedIn post about leadership."* → generic fluff.
- Good prompt: Role-play, attach rough ideas, ask for pushback and research.
- **Power moves when results disappoint:**
  - Give rich context (connect to Google Drive, email, calendar, Notion).
  - Ground insights by asking Claude to do deep web research — then verify.
  - Use AI to build your AI skill in real time (open a second chat to learn while doing).

### 2. Remember — Claude Projects
- Projects give Claude **persistent memory** across sessions: upload files, set instructions, define tone.
- Example: a career-change project with resume, LinkedIn profile, target job descriptions, writing samples, and a custom instruction brief.
- **Key insight:** Customize at the *project level*, not the account level — planning a vacation is different from planning retirement.

### 3. Execute — Claude Co-work
- A desktop app (Mac/Windows, paid plans) for **multi-step, file-based tasks**.
- Use when: assets are on your hard drive, task needs multiple steps, output needs to be a local file.
- Can connect to other AI tools via **MCP (Model Context Protocol)** — demonstrated with Hicksfield (creative AI) to generate ad creatives, videos, and images from a single prompt.
- **Security practice:** sandbox by creating a subfolder with only the files Claude needs.

### 4. Build — Claude Code
- You do **not** need a CS background to build with Claude Code.
- Describe the problem in English: *"I want a dashboard to see which deals are at risk, what's stuck, and my next move."*
- Biggest missed opportunity: assuming coding is only for engineers.

### 5. Browse — Claude Chrome Extension
- Sits in your browser alongside your workflow.
- Reads job listings, 40-page industry reports, forms, emails — in real time.
- Acts where most knowledge work already happens: the browser.

## The PRIME Prompting Framework

| Letter | Meaning | What to Do |
|--------|---------|------------|
| **P** | Purpose | Give a precise goal (e.g., "turn this messy proposal into a 5-part client memo") |
| **R** | Research | Ask Claude to fetch and verify external data; provide your own notes/transcripts |
| **I** | Interview | Let Claude ask you multiple-choice questions to refine its approach |
| **M** | Mechanics | Specify output format: bullets vs paragraph, concise vs detailed, strategic vs conversational |
| **E** | Examples | Show what good looks like — a past deck, a tone reference, an outline |

## Key Insights

1. **The human is the edge.** AI is only as good as the input you give it. Superusers direct Claude like they'd direct a smart new hire.

2. **Labels are outdated.** *"I'm not technical / I'm not a designer / I don't have an MBA"* — for ~$20/month, you can access capabilities that exceed most MBAs and PhDs. The real differentiator is curiosity, tenacity, and patience.

3. **AI levels the playing field.** A solo founder with the right stack can produce creative output that used to require a large team and budget (demonstrated with the candle business + Hicksfield example).

4. **Learn while doing.** Use a second Claude chat to learn concepts (e.g., IRR, spreadsheet features) while building in the first. The cycle of *learn-and-do, do-and-learn* compounds.

5. **The real question:** Not whether you're outgunned, but how you outshine when you are. AI can't give you innate curiosity — but you already have it.
